how it works

Where the fees go.

Most perp DEXs keep their fees, or send them to a vault that is the house and takes the other side of your trades. The Court is not the house. It never touches trader PnL. It only receives what the desk earns.

01You trade. Stocks, indices, commodities, FX and crypto perps on one balance, any hour of the week.
02The desk earns a fee on every fill. A second stream comes from swaps of $THRONE itself in its Uniswap pool.
03Fees route to two vaults. Senior takes USDG from anyone, earns base yield and is paid first. The Court sits behind it, absorbs losses first, and receives the desk's fees.
04The Court is gated. To take a seat you stake $THRONE alongside your deposit. Want the seat, hold the key.

Liquidations and bad debt are handled at the venue layer, outside both vaults. Paid in fees only. Fixed supply. No emissions anywhere in the design.

questions

The ones people actually ask.

Is the vault taking the other side of my trades?

No. Neither vault market-makes or backstops liquidations. Liquidations and bad debt are handled at the venue layer. The Court's exposure is to fee volume, not to trader PnL.

What does $THRONE actually do?

It is the key to the Court. To deposit into the Court you stake $THRONE alongside your USDG. No stake, no seat. It is not a governance token and it is not the yield itself.

What protects Senior?

Order of operations. Senior is paid first every epoch and the Court's capital absorbs losses before Senior sees them. Senior can withdraw any epoch. Withdrawals cannot be paused.

How do I get into the Court?

Hold $THRONE, trade the desk during season zero to earn points, and stake when the Court opens. Points decide priority. Seats are limited.

Where does the money come from?

Two places. Fees from real trading on the desk, and the 0.7% fee on $THRONE swaps in its Uniswap pool. Senior also earns base yield on its deposits. Nothing is paid from token emissions.

When does the desk open?

The beta is live for a first group of traders; reserve a seat. Public open follows. Season zero points start with the beta. The Court opens after the desk. @thronedefi is the only place anything is announced.

a note from the desk

Why this exists.

Every perp DEX I have used had the same quiet problem. The vault that paid me was also the house. When I traded well, it bled. When the yield was good, it was because someone else was losing. That is not a business, it is a casino with a dividend.

Throne is built the other way round. The desk is a real venue with real order books, stocks next to crypto, open when Wall Street is not. It earns a fee on every fill. That fee does not stay with us. It goes to the Court, and the Court is held by the people who hold $THRONE.

No emissions. No promises about yield. A fee stream, a small vault that collects it, and a token that is the only way in. If the desk trades, the seats get paid. If it does not, they do not.
