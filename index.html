questionsfol. 08
What is the Court?
A vault with two sides on Robinhood Chain. It collects fees, votes and emissions in one place and pays USDG depositors first, every week.
Where does the money come from?
Desk fees and pons swap fees today. Fables votes and the Bounty market once Fables' token is live. Every source is a row on the stats page.
Who takes the loss?
The Court's junior side, first. Senior has first claim on income and holds nothing that can lose principal except Morpho itself.
Why do I need $THRONE?
It is the key. Lock it for 16 weeks to point the votes, stake it alongside USDG to sit on the junior side.
When can I deposit?
Senior opens in November as a capped vault. Caps lift after audit.
