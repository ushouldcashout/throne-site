How points work

Full rules and the live board: the board
01 · SCORE
1 point per $10

Taker volume only, on any market on the desk. Points count from your first trade. The venue posts rows hourly; the board refreshes every ten minutes.

02 · MULTIPLY
Hold $THRONE, score more

Hold 100k through the week for 1.25x, 500k for 1.5x. Beta list wallets score 2x. Combined cap 2x. The week's lowest balance sets the tier.

03 · PIECES
Sixteen seats a side

King, queen, two rooks, two bishops, two knights, eight pawns, by season points. Seats change hands live. A wallet directly below you out-scoring you this week puts your piece under attack. You may switch sides once a season, and only in the first 48 hours of a week. After that, sides lock until the next week opens.

04 · CHECK IN & CLOSE
Check in daily, close Sunday

One free signature a day is +100 points, all seven days +300 more, counted once you clear the $20,000 floor. Weeks close Sunday midnight UTC (8pm ET). The final hour is power hour: points above your 11pm UTC baseline score 1.5x. Results publish with a hash.

WINNING SIDE
72%

Of the drop to every wallet on the winning side, split by that week's points. $20,000 taker volume in the week to be paid; points count either way.

WINNING PIECES
18%

To the winning side's sixteen pieces, weighted king to pawn. The king takes 16 shares, the queen 15, down to one share per pawn. Same $20,000 floor.

LOSING KING & QUEEN
10%

The losing side's king and queen still get paid, 7% and 3% of the drop. Leading your side is worth it either way.

$20,000 taker volume in a week is the floor to be paid from that week's drop; points, seats and check-ins count regardless. Rankings follow the venue's hourly public rows. Formula: points = taker volume x 0.1 x multiplier. Estimates use the published split and the current $THRONE price and are not a promise of payment. Figures are live until each week closes; the closed result is what pays. Names are chosen by wallets and signed; an X handle with a check was verified against a public post.
