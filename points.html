How points work

full rules and the live board: the board
01 · SCORE
1 point per $10

taker volume only, on any market on the desk. points count from your first trade. the venue posts rows hourly; the board refreshes every ten minutes.

02 · MULTIPLY
hold $THRONE, score more

hold 100k through the week for 1.25x, 500k for 1.5x. beta list wallets score 2x. combined cap 2x. the week's lowest balance sets the tier.

03 · PIECES
sixteen seats a side

king, queen, two rooks, two bishops, two knights, eight pawns, by season points. seats change hands live. a wallet directly below you out-scoring you this week puts your piece under attack. you may switch sides once a season, and only in the first 48 hours of a week. after that, sides lock until the next week opens.

04 · CLOSE
sunday 7pm est

weeks run friday to sunday close. from week 1 the final hour is power hour: points above your 6pm baseline score 1.5x. results publish with a hash on the board.

WINNING SIDE
72%

of the drop to every wallet on the winning side, split by that week's points. $6,969 taker volume in the week to qualify.

WINNING PIECES
18%

to the winning side's sixteen pieces, weighted king to pawn. the king takes 16 shares, the queen 15, down to one share per pawn.

LOSING KING & QUEEN
10%

the losing side's king and queen still get paid, 7% and 3% of the drop. leading your side is worth it either way.

rankings follow the venue's hourly public rows. formula: points = taker volume x 0.1 x multiplier. estimates use the published split and the current $THRONE price and are not a promise of payment. figures are live until each week closes; the closed result is what pays.
