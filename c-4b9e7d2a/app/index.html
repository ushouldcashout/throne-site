Wireframe notes for design. Three tabs, one layout: action card left, position card right, three-step strip under Senior only. Mint = Senior and anything that pays first; gold = the Court, lockers and anything that takes risk. All amounts in mono. No percentages anywhere except the fixed structural ones (20% stake, 50% locker share, 5% Bounty cut, 20% junior cap). Never an APY, never a projection. Buttons carry their status in the label ("· not live") so no modal is needed. Connect sits in the nav, right side, and is the only thing that changes the right-hand cards. Mobile: tabs scroll horizontally, cards stack action-then-position.
