A Bounty is tokens deposited against (pool, epoch) in one escrow contract. At epoch close, every voter's share of the block is snapshotted, the payout is published as a Merkle root, and voters claim. Minimum 250 USDG. The Court's 5% is taken at settlement and shows on the ledger the same day.

how it settles4 steps
  1. 01 post A project posts tokens into escrow against a pool and an epoch.
  2. 02 vote Sink casts one vote a week; vlTHRONE lockers decide where the Court's weight inside that block points.
  3. 03 settle Every voter's share is snapshotted at close; 95% of each Bounty is split by it; a Merkle root is published.
