Want the chain's liquidity? Pay the voters.

Fables pays emissions to whichever pools the voters pick. Bounty is where a project puts money on the table for those votes. Sink's block follows the best-paying pools, the voters who make up the block get paid, and the Court keeps 5% for running the room. Votium did this on Curve and paid out hundreds of millions. A Bounty is tokens deposited against (pool, epoch) in one escrow contract. At epoch close, every voter's share of the block is snapshotted, the payout is published as a Merkle root, and voters claim. Minimum 250 USDG. The Court's 5% is taken at settlement and shows on the ledger the same day.

Snapshot and root

Every voter's share of the weight is snapshotted at close. 95% of each Bounty is split by it; a Merkle root is published.

Pull-claim for voters. The 5% lands on the Court's ledger tagged "bounty", and flows down the waterfall.
