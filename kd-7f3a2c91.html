How it works
01Every wallet that trades is in. You're assigned a side; pick one once a season to make it yours.

03Weeks close Sunday 7pm EST; the side with more points takes the King Drop.
04Top 16 on each side hold the pieces. Points set your place in line when the Court opens.
05Results are published with a hash so anyone can check them against the venue's public data.
