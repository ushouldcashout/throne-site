How it works
01Every wallet that trades is in. You're assigned a side; pick one once a season to make it yours.
02Trade the desk; taker volume earns points for you and your side. 1 point per $10.
03Weeks close Sunday 7pm EST; the side with more points takes the King Drop, split by points. The losing side's king and queen still get paid: 10% of the drop, 7/3 between them. The final hour before the close is power hour: every point counts 1.5x.
04Hold $THRONE through the week and your points multiply: 100k for 1.25x, 500k for 1.5x, measured on your lowest balance of the week. Beta list wallets score 2x. Combined cap 2x.
05Top 16 on each side hold the pieces. Points set your place in line when the Court opens.
06Results are published with a hash so anyone can check them against the venue's public data.
